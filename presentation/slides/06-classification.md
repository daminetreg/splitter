## Not every body  
is movable.

::: cards 2
- split | accent | Ordinary emitted definitions become pieces.
- keep | split | main · templates · virtuals · constructors · destructors · internal header linkage · macro-bound extents · not emitted
:::

::: rationale
Rationale — Keep forms whose semantics depend on instantiation, linkage, class syntax, or unreplayable macro context rather than approximating them.
:::
